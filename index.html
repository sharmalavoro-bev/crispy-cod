Our Story

One counter.
Every craving.

Crispy Cod Fish Bar is Wolverhampton's all-in-one takeaway on Showell Road — proper fish & chips fried to order, stone-baked pizzas, char-grilled kebabs, hand-pressed burgers, bakery-fresh pies and Southern Fried Chicken, all cooked fresh behind the same counter. Whatever the table's arguing about for tea, we've got it covered.

  • Fresh, not frozen-and-forgotten. Cod is filleted and battered fresh, pizza dough is stretched to order.
  • Something for everyone. Veggie pizzas, veggie burgers, kids' meals with a free shot drink, family boxes for the table.
  • Eat in a hurry or feed the whole house. From a £4 kiddies meal to a £22.99 Special Family box.
